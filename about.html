Karis Missions International

Grace received becomes grace shared

That sentence is the whole ministry. Everything below is what it looks like in practice.

Start with the name

Karis comes from the Greek charis: grace. Not grace as a mood or a manner, but the specific, undeserved kindness of God toward people who had no claim on it.

If that is what we have received, it settles how we treat everyone else. It is why we go as learners rather than rescuers, why the resources on this site are free, and why we would rather tell you plainly what is still being built than impress you with what is not.

Our mark says the same thing without words. A lighthouse, because the light is not ours and our job is only to hold it where it can be seen. Cupped hands, because a gift you have been handed is carried carefully and offered on, not clutched.

Our mission

To proclaim Jesus Christ, make disciples, equip believers, strengthen local churches, and mobilize people to serve communities and nations through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Three convictions we will not trade

  1. 01

    The Gospel is for everyone

    People cannot believe in someone they have never heard of. Our first work is to make Christ known clearly, honestly, and without manipulation.

  2. 02

    The local church is central

    Mission that bypasses the local church leaves nothing behind. We work with the believers who will still be there long after a team has gone home.

  3. 03

    Disciples multiply

    A decision is a beginning, not an ending. We measure fruit by people who grow, who serve, and who go on to disciple somebody else.

What we actually run

I Share That You May Know

Our teaching and discipleship ministry: devotions sent daily to your phone, written articles, Bible teaching on video, and books. Everything is organised by Scripture, topic, and discipleship stage rather than piled up by date.

Know & Grow

Africa Missions

Short-term teams, supervised student internships, and professional service alongside local churches, with Ghana as our initial field. Placements run from one week to three months, with references, screening, and safeguarding preparation before anyone travels.

Our missions work

Missions for Children

Reaching children in the places we serve, and helping the children already among us pray, give, and go at the level they are actually at. Safeguarding comes before everything else on that page, and we say so plainly.

Missions for Children

Karis Academy

Structured Christian formation across five pathways: Foundations, Discipleship, Ministry, Missions, and Leadership. Built around formation rather than information, and honest that certificates are ministry credentials, not accredited degrees.

The Resource Center

Finished gospel tracts and outreach artwork, free to download, print, and reproduce. The Word versions let you swap our contact details for your own church's, so what you hand out points people back to you.

Governance and accountability

Karis is being structured for accountability, stewardship, and long-term continuity. As the ministry grows, oversight is organised across the areas listed here.

We would rather be plain about our stage than imply we are further along than we are. Where a structure is still being built, we say so on the page rather than in a footnote.
