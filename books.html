Books & Publications

Christ-centered publications for discipleship, leadership, missions, evangelism, and Christian living.

What we are writing

Karis publications are written to be used, not only read, each one connected to study questions, related teaching, and a practical next step.

In development

I Share That You May Know

The book behind our teaching ministry: why every believer can share Christ clearly, and how to begin without pressure, gimmicks, or fear.

In development

The Story of Redemption

A biblical theology project tracing the thread of Scripture from Creation and covenant, through Christ and the Church, to mission and new creation.

Ministry resource

Missionary Handbook

Training material for Karis missionaries: spiritual preparation, cross-cultural ministry, Ghana field service, evangelism, and team life. Issued to accepted applicants.

Published and Free Today

While the books are in development, our printed evangelism material is finished, tested in real outreach, and free to download and reproduce.

Three complete gospel tracts

Each tract is a full Gospel presentation in its own right, written plainly, grounded in Scripture, and finished to print standard. They are supplied as PDFs for printing and as Word documents so your own assembly's details can be substituted.

  • You May Not Feel Lost… But You Need Rescue: for people who are comfortable but disconnected from God.
  • Are You Ready to Meet Your Maker?: for thoughtful sceptics and open-air conversations.
  • The Call to Truly Believe: a fuller presentation, useful as follow-up for new believers.
