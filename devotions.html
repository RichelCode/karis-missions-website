I Share That You May Know

A word from Scripture, every day

Our devotions go out on WhatsApp, so they reach you where you already are. Free to follow, and you can leave whenever you want.

Follow the channel

Rather than asking you to remember to visit a website, we send the devotion to you.

Each one takes about four minutes to read: a passage of Scripture, a short reflection, one honest question, and a prayer. They are written to be used consistently rather than admired occasionally, and to be forwarded to someone who needs them.

You will not be asked to reply, perform, or keep up. Some days you will read it and some days you will not. That is fine.

What arrives

  • A passage: read in context, not lifted out of it.
  • A reflection: plain, practical, and honest about the cost of obedience.
  • A question: one thing to actually do or consider today.
  • A prayer: short enough to pray, specific enough to mean.

Karis Devotions on WhatsApp

Tap once to follow. The next devotion arrives with your other messages, and nothing else does.

Follow on WhatsApp
  • Free, and it always will be
  • Nobody can see your number, not even us
  • Other followers cannot see you or message you
  • Mute it or leave any time, without telling anyone

Why a phone and not a page? Because the devotion you actually read is the one that reaches you on a Tuesday morning when you are already holding your phone, not the one waiting on a website you meant to visit.
