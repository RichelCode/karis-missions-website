Little Lights · The Light Comes Down · Day 3 of 3

The shepherds tell everyone

Good news is for sharing!

From the Bible: Luke 2:8-20

The story

Out in the fields near Bethlehem, some shepherds were looking after their sheep in the dark. It was a quiet, ordinary night.

Suddenly an angel appeared, and the glory of the Lord shone all around them! The shepherds were terrified.

But the angel said, "Don't be afraid! I bring you good news of great joy for all the people. Today a Saviour has been born!" Then the whole sky filled with angels praising God.

The shepherds hurried to Bethlehem and found baby Jesus, just as the angel had said. They were so happy that they told everyone they met about Him!

The shepherds were not rich or famous. But God chose them to be the first to hear the good news, and they could not keep it to themselves. You can share the good news of Christmas, too.

The big idea

Good news is for sharing!

Memory verse

“Don’t be afraid, for behold, I bring you good news of great joy which will be to all the people.”

Luke 2:10

Say it three times: first in a whisper, then in your normal voice, then nice and loud!

Talk about it

  • How did the shepherds feel when they saw the angel? How did they feel at the end?
  • Why do you think God told shepherds first?
  • Who could you tell about Jesus this Christmas?

Try this

Make Christmas cards for neighbours or friends. Inside, write or draw the good news: Jesus is born! Deliver them together with a grown-up.

Let's pray

Dear God, thank You for the good news of Jesus. Give me joy like the shepherds, and help me to share it with someone this Christmas. Amen.

For grown-ups. Christmas is the easiest time of year for a child to talk about Jesus, because the whole world is already singing about Him. Help them take one simple step of telling.
