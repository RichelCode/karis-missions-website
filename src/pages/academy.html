Every Course Asks Four Questions

Biblical truth

What does Scripture actually teach about this?

Spiritual formation

How should this shape my life with Christ?

Practical ministry

How do I live and serve differently because of it?

Multiplication

How do I help another person grow in the same thing?

Who It Is For

New and growing believers

Anyone who wants a clear, unhurried grounding in the faith rather than a scattering of sermons and videos.

Volunteers and missionaries

Preparation before you travel, and formation that continues after you return.

Church and ministry leaders

Material you can use to develop the people you lead, not only to develop yourself.

Credential integrity. Karis Academy certificates are ministry formation credentials. They are not accredited academic degrees or professional qualifications, and we will never present them as such unless and until such status is formally established. We would rather be plain about this now than have anyone misled later.
