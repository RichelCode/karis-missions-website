What to Expect

Come as you are

There is no dress code and no expectation that you already know how any of it works. Sit at the back if you would rather not be noticed. That is completely fine.

Bring your questions

Doubts and hard questions are welcome. If you would like to talk with someone afterwards, ask anyone. They will point you to a leader.

You will not be pressured

You will not be singled out, put on the spot, or asked to give. Come, listen, and decide in your own time.

Planning to visit? Read what to expect on your first visit, or see all events and service times. Service times can change for special events and holidays. Call the assembly directly, or send us a message and we will confirm before you travel.
