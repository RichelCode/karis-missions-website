Where we stand

Karis Missions International stands within historic, orthodox Christian faith. We hold these truths not as a formality but as the ground we stand on, in our teaching, in our published resources, and in every field where we serve.

01Scripture

We affirm the Holy Bible, the sixty-six books of the Old and New Testaments, as the inspired and authoritative Word of God, entirely trustworthy and the final standard for faith and practice.

Where Scripture speaks, we submit. Where it is silent, we hold our opinions humbly.

02The Triune God

We believe in one eternal God, existing in three Persons (Father, Son, and Holy Spirit), equal in power and glory, distinct in Person, undivided in essence. He is the Creator and Sustainer of all things, holy, sovereign, righteous, and abounding in mercy.

03Jesus Christ

We affirm the deity of Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, His sinless life, His atoning death on the cross for our sins, His bodily resurrection, His ascension, His present reign, and His personal return in glory. He is fully God and fully man, and there is salvation in no one else.

“These things are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and by believing you may have life in His name.” John 20:31

04The Holy Spirit

We affirm the Person and work of the Holy Spirit, who convicts the world of sin, regenerates and indwells every believer, produces Christ-like character, and empowers the Church for witness and service.

We affirm the spiritual gifts He distributes according to His will, exercised in love, in order, and in submission to Scripture.

05Salvation

Salvation is God's gracious gift, received through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ alone. It is not earned by human effort, religious observance, or moral achievement. Those who trust in Christ are forgiven, justified, adopted into God's family, and being made holy by the Spirit.

Would you like to know how to begin? Our free gospel tracts explain this plainly, with a prayer you can pray today. Read or download them here.

06The Church

We affirm the Church as the body of Christ, made up of all true believers, and expressed locally in congregations that gather for worship, the Word, prayer, fellowship, the ordinances, and mission.

We believe the local church is God's primary instrument in the world. We work to strengthen local churches rather than to replace or compete with them.

07The Great Commission

We affirm the calling of every believer to participate in the Great Commission: making disciples of all nations, baptising them, and teaching them to obey everything Christ commanded. Mission is not a department of the Church. It is the Church living out its purpose.

“Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you.” Matthew 28:19, 20

08Last Things

We affirm the personal, bodily return of Jesus Christ, the resurrection of the dead, the final judgment, the eternal blessedness of the redeemed, and the new heavens and new earth in which righteousness dwells.

This hope shapes how we live, how we serve, and why we share Christ with both urgency and compassion.

On secondary matters

Christians who hold to these core convictions may still differ sincerely on secondary questions. Karis works with churches and partners across a range of evangelical traditions.

We ask everyone serving with us to hold their differences with grace, with charity, and with respect for local church leadership. A team that argues its way through a village helps nobody.
