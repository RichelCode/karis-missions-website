Help take the Gospel further

Your generosity supports missionary preparation, free Gospel resources, leadership development, local-church ministry, volunteer assistance, and community outreach.

Every resource in our Resource Center is free to download and reproduce because someone chose to fund it rather than sell it. That is what giving does here.

Where gifts go

  • Missionary support: preparing and sustaining people sent to serve.
  • Volunteer scholarships: helping willing applicants for whom cost is the only barrier.
  • Gospel resources: writing, designing, and printing tracts and outreach material.
  • Leadership training: developing local leaders who will still be there in ten years.
  • Community outreach: practical service alongside local churches.

Our Commitment

Stewardship you can check

We will report honestly on how gifts are used, avoid inflated impact claims, and never pressure anyone to give. Giving is a response to God's grace, not a transaction with a ministry.

Questions About Giving

Is my gift tax-deductible?
Tax treatment depends on the ministry entity receiving your gift and on where you live. Please ask us before you give and we will tell you exactly what applies in your case rather than give you a general answer that might be wrong for you.
Can I give to something specific?
Yes. Designated gifts are honoured for the purpose given. If a designated need is fully met, we will contact you before applying your gift anywhere else.
Will I be added to a mailing list?
Only if you ask to be. Giving does not sign you up for anything, and we do not share or sell donor information. See our Privacy Policy.
I cannot give financially. Can I still help?
Very much so. Prayer, volunteering, hosting a team, sharing our tracts, or lending professional skills are all genuine contributions. Start with the Prayer Center or Partner page.
