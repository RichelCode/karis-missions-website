The Karis Framework

Five steps. We mean all five.

Most ministries are good at one or two of these. We build every initiative to move a person to the next one. A decision is a beginning, not a result.

  1. 1

    Know

    Encounter Jesus Christ through the Gospel.

  2. 2

    Grow

    Be transformed by Scripture, prayer, and the Holy Spirit.

  3. 3

    Serve

    Use your gifts to strengthen the Church and bless others.

  4. 4

    Go

    Take part in God's mission locally and globally.

  5. 5

    Multiply

    Make disciples who go on to make disciples.

Field Principles

We serve with local churches, not around them.

  • We enter another culture as learners, not rescuers.
  • We protect dignity, privacy, and appropriate boundaries.
  • We report outcomes honestly, without inflated statistics.
