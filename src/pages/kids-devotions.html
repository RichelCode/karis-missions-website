Hello, Little Light!

Jesus said, “You are the light of the world.” That means you! These Bible stories will show you how to shine.

Each day has a Bible story to read with a grown-up, a big idea to remember, a verse to learn, something fun to do, and a prayer. When you finish a day, press I did it! to collect your star.

For parents and teachers

At home

Ten minutes, side by side

Read the story aloud, or press Read it to me and listen together. Then talk through the questions. There are no wrong answers, only conversations worth having.

In class

A week of Sunday school

One story per session, or a whole series as a holiday club. Each page prints cleanly on its own, picture and all, and the colouring book gives every child something to take home.

The thread

Know, Grow, Serve, Go, Multiply

Five Steps with Jesus follows the Karis Framework, so children walk the same path as the adults around them, at their own size.

About these devotions. Scripture is quoted from the World English Bible, which is free to share. The stars children collect are kept only on their own device; nothing is sent to us, and there is nothing to sign up for. More series are being written. If you use these with children and have ideas, we would love to hear them.
