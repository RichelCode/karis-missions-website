I Share That You May Know

The teaching and discipleship ministry of Karis Missions International.

Our purpose is simple: to share biblical truth in accessible, practical ways so that people may know Jesus Christ, grow in their relationship with Him, and become equipped to share Him with others.

Rather than publishing disconnected posts, we organise teaching by Scripture, topic, and discipleship stage, so each resource helps a person take a real next step rather than simply consuming more content.

“How then will they call on Him in whom they have not believed? And how are they to believe in Him of whom they have never heard? And how are they to hear without someone preaching?”
