Seminaries & Bible Colleges

Develop supervised field education, mission internships, student evaluation, and cross-cultural formation.

Organizations & Businesses

Contribute expertise, services, sponsorship, logistics, technology, and responsible project support.

Individuals

Pray, volunteer, give, advocate, mentor, or use your professional skills for Kingdom service.

What Partnership Looks Like

It is mutual

Partnership is not a donor relationship in one direction. Local churches shape what we do; partners bring gifts we do not have; everyone gives and everyone receives.

It is specific

We would rather agree on one clear, achievable commitment than sign a broad memorandum that nobody acts on.

It is accountable

We report back on what happened, including what did not work. Partners should never have to guess what their support achieved.
