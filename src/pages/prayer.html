Prayer is not a support department

Prayer is not something that happens behind the mission of Karis Missions International. Prayer is part of the mission.

Whatever you are carrying (a decision, a diagnosis, a family, a fear, a person you have prayed for over years), you can bring it here. You do not have to explain it well. You do not have to be a member of anything.

Ways to pray with us

  • Submit a personal prayer request, named or anonymous.
  • Pray for current mission needs and the local churches we serve.
  • Share a praise report of answered prayer.
  • Join us in person: midweek prayer meets every week.

Your request is handled with care. Requests are read by ministry leaders only. Nothing is published, shared publicly, or posted on social media without your explicit permission. See our Privacy Policy.

If you are in crisis right now

We will pray with you, and we want to be honest that prayer and professional help are not opposites. If you are in immediate danger or thinking of harming yourself, please contact your local emergency services. In the United States you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, at any hour.
