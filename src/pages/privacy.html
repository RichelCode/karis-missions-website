Karis Missions International respects the trust involved when someone shares a prayer request, an application, or a personal question with us. This policy explains in plain language what happens to that information.

Last updated: this policy is maintained alongside the website. Contact us if you need the date of a specific revision.

What We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us through a form on this site. Depending on the form, that may include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and country or city.
  • Information about your church involvement, faith journey, skills, and availability (volunteer application).
  • Prayer requests, which may include sensitive personal circumstances.
  • Details of a partnership, giving, or resource enquiry.

We do not collect payment card details through this website.

Why We Use It

  • To reply to your message or enquiry.
  • To pray for the requests we receive.
  • To assess, support, and prepare volunteer and mission applicants.
  • To send you information you have specifically asked to receive.

We do not use your information for anything else, and we will not add you to a mailing list because you contacted us about something unrelated.

Prayer Requests

Prayer requests are read by ministry leaders only. Nothing you send is published, shared publicly, read aloud, or posted on social media without your explicit permission. If you would like your request treated as strictly confidential to one leader, say so in the request and we will honour it.

Sensitive Information

Some forms invite information that is sensitive by nature, health circumstances, family situations, or matters of faith. Please share only what you are comfortable sharing. Where a mission placement requires health or travel documentation, we will tell you specifically what is needed and why before you provide it.

Information About Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If a child has submitted information through this site, contact us and we will delete it. Consent from a parent or guardian is required before any young person participates in ministry activity or appears in published photography.

Who Can See It

Your information is seen only by the Karis leaders and volunteers who need it to do the thing you asked for. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it with other organizations for their own purposes.

We use third-party services to operate the website and receive form submissions. Those providers process data on our behalf under their own security obligations. We will name the current provider on request.

How Long We Keep It

  • General enquiries: kept while we are in correspondence, then removed.
  • Prayer requests: retained only as long as needed to pray and follow up.
  • Volunteer applications: retained for the duration of the process and for a reasonable period afterwards for safeguarding and reference purposes.
  • Giving enquiries: retained where financial record-keeping obligations require it.

Your Choices

You may ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your information.
  • Stop contacting you.

Email tackiausa@gmail.com and we will act on your request. You never need to give a reason.

Videos

On our Watch page, videos are shown as a still picture with a play button. Those pictures are served from this website, so nothing is requested from YouTube, and no YouTube cookie is set, unless you choose to press play.

When you do press play, we load the player from youtube-nocookie.com, Google's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube will then receive your IP address and information about the video you are watching, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that. If you would rather not, simply do not press play, or use the "Open on YouTube" links only when you intend to.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads.

Security

We take reasonable care to protect the information entrusted to us. No website or email system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send highly sensitive information such as financial account details through a web form.
