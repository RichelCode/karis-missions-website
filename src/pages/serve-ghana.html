Field Principles

  • We serve with local churches, not around them.
  • We enter another culture as learners, not rescuers.
  • We protect dignity, privacy, and appropriate boundaries.
  • We report ministry outcomes responsibly and avoid inflated statistics.
  • Evangelism should connect people to ongoing discipleship wherever possible.
  • We do not photograph or publish anyone, least of all children, without informed consent.

Common Questions

What does it cost?
Participants are normally responsible for their own airfare, visa, insurance, vaccinations, in-country accommodation, and food. Exact figures depend on your departure city, the length of your placement, and the season, so we confirm a written estimate with you before you commit to anything. Ask us for a current estimate and we will not guess. We will find out.
Do I need to raise support?
Many participants raise support through their home church, family, and friends. We can supply a letter of endorsement and material to help you explain the trip. Where cost is the only barrier for an otherwise well-prepared applicant, please tell us. We would rather have that conversation than lose you.
How long are placements?
Placements range from one week to three months, with longer placements developing as the work matures. Internships are usually arranged around a school's academic requirements.
What screening is involved?
Applicants complete an interview, provide references including one from their local church, and complete safeguarding orientation before travel. Where a placement involves contact with children or vulnerable adults, additional background checks apply. This protects the people we serve and it protects you.
Do I need to be an experienced missionary?
No. You need a genuine walk with Christ, a teachable attitude, involvement in a local church, and a willingness to serve where you are needed rather than where you had pictured yourself. Skills matter less than character and humility.
Can my whole church send a team?
Yes. We work with churches, seminaries, and Bible colleges to prepare and send teams, including pre-trip training and post-trip debriefing. Start on our Partner page.
