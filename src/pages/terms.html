Using Our Resources

The tracts, outreach artwork, and study material in our Resource Center exist to be used. We would rather they were in circulation than protected.

You are free to

  • Download, print, and distribute the resources for ministry purposes, at no cost.
  • Reproduce them in any quantity your outreach requires.
  • Use the editable Word versions to substitute your own church's name, address, and service times.
  • Translate a resource into another language for local use.

We ask that you do not

  • Sell the material, or include it in anything sold for profit.
  • Alter the Gospel content, Scripture quotations, or doctrinal substance.
  • Present the material as your own original work.
  • Use the Karis Missions International name or logo in a way that implies endorsement, partnership, or affiliation that has not been agreed in writing.

Translating something? Please let us know. We would like to make good translations available to others rather than have the same work done five times over.

Logos and Brand Files

The Karis Missions International logo files are provided so that partner churches, seminaries, and mission teams can produce materials for work we are genuinely involved in. Please keep the marks unaltered, preserve their proportions, and do not recolour them or combine them with other logos into a new mark. If you are unsure whether a use is appropriate, ask us. We will almost always say yes.

Website Content

Text, photography, and design on this website remain the property of Karis Missions International unless stated otherwise. You may quote from the site with attribution and a link. Photographs of identifiable individuals may not be reused outside this site without our permission, because the consent given by those individuals was given to us.

Accuracy

We work to keep service times, locations, costs, and ministry information accurate and current. Details can change, particularly around holidays and special events, so please confirm with us or with the relevant assembly before travelling any distance.

Where a programme is still being developed, we say so on the page rather than describing it as though it were already running.

External Links

This site links to third-party services such as map providers and social media platforms. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those services, and a link is not an endorsement of everything on the site it points to.

No Professional Advice

Material on this site is pastoral and educational. It is not legal, medical, financial, immigration, or psychological advice, and it should not be relied upon in place of a qualified professional.
