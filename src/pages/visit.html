Sunday in Colorado Springs

Most first-time visitors come to Karis International Assembly on a Sunday morning. The service runs from 10:00am to about 12:00pm.

Arriving ten minutes early gives you time to find a seat without hurrying. If you would like someone to look out for you, tell us below and a member of the church will meet you at the door. If you would rather slip in unnoticed, that is completely fine too.

What happens in a service

  • Worship: singing together. Join in, or simply listen.
  • Prayer: for the church, for the world, and for people who have asked for it.
  • Scripture and preaching: the Bible read and explained, applied to everyday life.
  • Fellowship: people tend to stay and talk afterwards. Stay as long or as short as you like.

The congregation is international, gathered from many nations. Whoever you are and wherever you are from, you will be welcome.

Questions People Ask

What should I wear?
Whatever you are comfortable in. Some people dress up and some come in jeans. Nobody will notice, and nobody will mind.
Can I bring my children?
Yes, please do. Children are part of the gathered church here, not an interruption to it, and a little noise is welcome. If you would like to know what is offered for children at a particular assembly, ask us before you come. For home, our free Little Lights devotions and picture book are made to be read together.
Will I be asked to stand up, speak, or give money?
No. You will not be singled out, put on the spot, or asked to give. Come, listen, and decide in your own time.
I am not sure what I believe. Is that all right?
Completely. Doubts and hard questions are welcome. If you would like to talk with someone afterwards, ask anyone and they will point you to a leader. You can also read what we believe before you come.
Is there parking, and is the building accessible?
If you have questions about parking, step-free access, or anything else that would make your visit easier, call +1 (719) 678-7888 or let us know below and we will make sure you are looked after.
I live in Denver, Greeley, or Delaware. Is there somewhere near me?
Yes. Karis Missions International serves alongside four assemblies of The Apostolic Church International, USA. See all places of worship for addresses, times, and who to call.

Let us know you are coming

Entirely optional. Tell us when you plan to visit and we will have someone look out for you, save you a seat, and answer any questions.
