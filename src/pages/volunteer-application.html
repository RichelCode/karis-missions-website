Before you begin

This application is an expression of interest, not a guarantee of placement. Approved applicants move through review, an interview, references, preparation, and then a placement offer.

It takes about ten minutes. Write plainly. We are not looking for polished answers, we are looking for an honest picture of where you are.

What happens after you submit

  1. We acknowledge your application, usually within a week.
  2. If it looks like a fit, we arrange a conversation.
  3. We request references, including one from your local church.
  4. You complete orientation and safeguarding preparation.
  5. We confirm a placement, dates, and a written cost estimate.

How we handle your information. What you write here is used only to assess and support your application. We do not sell or share it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Safeguarding commitments.
