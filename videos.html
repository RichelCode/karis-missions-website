Have a story worth filming?

If you have served with Karis, been part of an outreach, or have a testimony you would be willing to record, we would like to hear from you. Field stories told by the people who lived them are worth more than anything we could script.

We will always ask permission before publishing anyone's image or story, and we will never use footage of a child without the written consent of a parent or guardian.
